01ABOUT THE COMMUNITY

ONE COMMUNITY.
ENDLESS POSSIBILITIES.

AWS Student Builder Group at SB Jain Institute of Technology is a student-led technology community focused on cloud computing, AWS, DevOps, artificial intelligence and modern software development.

Our goal is to create a space where students can learn together, build real projects, participate in technical events and connect with technology professionals.
